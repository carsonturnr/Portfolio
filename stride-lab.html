Stride Lab

pre-launch · launching 2027

AI running-form analysis from phone video. Runners upload treadmill, workout or race footage and get a research-backed breakdown of injury risk, efficiency, symmetry and fatigue.

problem

Runners can’t easily see where their form breaks down, especially when they’re tired or racing. Lab gait analysis is expensive, takes time and doesn’t work on race footage.

status

Pre-launch. The landing page and waitlist are live and the product is fully specified. The analysis pipeline will be built after Mosaic, with a first release to a small group of runners planned for early 2027.

what it will do

  • Detection and tracking, so a runner can tap themselves once in group footage.
  • 2D pose estimation, camera-view classification and foot-contact detection.
  • Eleven metrics from published biomechanics research across injury risk, efficiency and foot strike, each computed only from camera angles where it’s valid.
  • Left-right symmetry and fatigue drift, compared against the runner’s own baseline.
  • A coach that recommends drills based on the measured numbers and doesn’t diagnose injuries.

stack

Built: React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase and Vercel. Planned: Python GPU workers on Modal, a YOLO-class detector with ByteTrack-style tracking, RTMPose, Claude and Stripe.
