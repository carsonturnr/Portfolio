Rapha

iOS · 2025

An iOS app for young adults that builds a personalized 28-day devotional plan from their onboarding answers, with daily content, a faith chat and a daily-verse widget.

problem

Generic devotionals rarely fit a person’s life stage, struggles or faith background, which makes a daily habit hard to keep.

what i built

  • A Claude pipeline that outlines the full 28-day plan in one call, then generates each week’s seven days in parallel.
  • A job queue inside Postgres: creating a plan queues the later weeks, and pg_cron runs them on schedule with retries.
  • A feedback loop where end-of-cycle ratings and preferences shape the next plan.
  • Context-aware prompts that draw on the user’s profile, the season and the liturgical calendar.
  • A SwiftUI app of about 150 views with a 15-question onboarding flow, Apple and Google sign-in, a paywall and a WidgetKit verse widget.

how it works

The app calls a Supabase Edge Function rather than the model directly. One call returns the plan’s themes and all 28 day titles, so the month is planned as a whole. Day 1 is generated first and the rest of the week follows in the background with Day 1 as context. Later weeks see earlier verses and lesson excerpts so content doesn’t repeat, and the daily verse skips anything used in the last 60 days.

stack

Swift, SwiftUI and WidgetKit. Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Edge Functions in TypeScript, pg_cron, pg_net). Claude. Superwall.
