Sweet-Haus

client · 2026

A custom cabin configurator and a unified marketing site for Sweet-Haus, a tiny-home and cabin builder in Chesnee, South Carolina.

the ask

Sweet-Haus wanted customers to design and price a cabin online, with every request reaching the sales team as a structured lead. Their marketing site also ran on another development company’s CMS, so they wanted it brought in-house.

what i built

  • A configurator that swaps between 181 real renders across 7 models as customers choose paint, roof and trim, falling back to the closest render when a combination doesn’t exist.
  • A pricing engine covering base price, options and add-ons, with a monthly payment calculator and a shipping estimate based on distance.
  • A lead pipeline that saves each build, syncs it to Airtable and hands it to the proposal portal, which emails the customer a link to their build.
  • An admin area for models, options, pricing and submissions, with CSV export.
  • A migration of 33 pages from the old site, with 301 redirects, a sitemap and search metadata.

how it works

Renders follow a naming convention and a build step indexes the filenames, so the image files themselves decide which combinations are available. Models, options and prices live in Supabase and are edited in the admin area, with a bundled data file as a fallback.

by the numbers

  • 9 cabin models
  • 527 option choices
  • 181 rendered color combinations
  • Launched September 2026

stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind. Supabase. Resend and Airtable. Vercel.
