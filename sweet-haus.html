Sweet-Haus

client · 2026

A site and cabin configurator for Sweet-Haus, a maker of luxury tiny cabins. Buyers design their cabin and see the price as they go.

[ screenshot ]

the ask

Sweet-Haus wanted buyers and dealers to design a cabin online, see accurate pricing as they go and submit a ready-to-quote request.

what i built

  • A configurator that swaps the cabin render live as you pick paint, trim and roof.
  • Live itemised pricing with add-ons and setup options.
  • A dealer mode with its own pricing.
  • An admin area for models, options, pricing, dealers and submissions, with CSV export.

stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind.
