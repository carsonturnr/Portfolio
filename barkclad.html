Barkclad

client · 2026

A new website, online store and internal Command Center for BarkClad, a North Carolina maker of patented poplar bark siding, all on one shared database.

the ask

BarkClad ran on several disconnected platforms, including a WordPress site and a separate SmartBark site. They needed one system, without risking the search rankings the old sites had earned.

what i built

  • About 40 public pages, including product pages, a product-chooser quiz, project galleries and an installation guide.
  • A working store with Stripe payments, North Carolina sales tax by county, and live shipping rates and labels through Shippo.
  • A CMS with 10 content types and 23 editable page sections, so staff can change pages, products and prices with drafts and live preview.
  • The Command Center: one private dashboard for every lead, quote, order and customer, with a sales pipeline board, quoting, invoicing, label buying and a daily check that flags overdue follow-ups.
  • Role-based access for admin, editor and sales users.

keeping search rankings

167 old URLs, including legacy WordPress links and the entire SmartBark domain, redirect to their new pages. Original titles, descriptions and headings were carried over, 45 blog posts were migrated, and search traffic held after the switch.

stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and Payload CMS. Supabase. Stripe, Shippo and Resend. Vercel.
